## 🎨 Design System & Visual Tokens Reference

- **Theme Name**: Cosmic Bubble & Star Soda
- **Primary Hue**: Neon Magenta / Bubble Pink (`#F72585`)
- **Secondary Hue**: Star Glow Violet (`#9D4EDD`)
- **Accent Hue**: Nebula Cyan / Aqua Glow (`#4CC9F0`)
- **Highlight Hue**: Star Honey Yellow (`#FFD166`)
- **Background Tones**: Light Celestial Pastel (`#FEF7FF`, `#FAF0FF`)
- **Card / Surface Roundness**: `ROUND_FULL` (Pill & Clay rounded radius: `24px`–`32px`)
- **Art Direction**: 3D tactile claymorphism with jelly-glass highlights, miniature isometric playset perspective, rich volumetric studio lighting, kid-safe UI with high contrast borders.
